Method and limitations

The sample contains remote roles returned by the Himalayas public Jobs API and filtered for India eligibility. It is not a census of all jobs in India and does not establish that a role is based in Chennai or on-site.

Skill shares count keyword matches in listing content. Salary values preserve the reported currency and pay period. Raw job descriptions and listing-level exports are not published.
